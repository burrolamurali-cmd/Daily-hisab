Privacy Policy

DailyHisaab — Daily Cash Book & Expense Tracker

Last updated: September 28, 2026

In short: DailyHisaab does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal or financial data to us or to any third party. Everything you enter — your income, expenses, savings, loans, PIN, and settings — stays only on your own device.

1. Who we are

DailyHisaab ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal daily income and expense tracking application for Android, developed by B Murali. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. DailyHisaab is designed to work completely offline. Specifically:

  • The App does not require an internet connection to function.
  • The App does not have any server, backend, or cloud database.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, or have access to any data you enter in the App.
  • The App does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, or any third-party analytics or advertising SDK.
  • The App does not display advertisements.

3. Data you enter into the App

When you use DailyHisaab, you may enter the following information, which is stored only in local storage on your own device (using Android's local database, Room/SQLite):

  • Daily income and expense entries, amounts, categories, and remarks/notes you type
  • Savings records and loan records (money you lent or borrowed), including amounts, dates, interest rates, and payments
  • Optional details of the person on the other side of a loan, if you choose to type them: name, father's name, village, mandal, district, state, and mobile number. These are only the details you enter yourself; the App never reads your phone contacts
  • A 4-digit PIN you set to lock the App
  • Your selected country, currency, and language preference
  • Your choice to show or hide the Savings & Loans tab

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, not shared with us, and not shared with any third party. If you uninstall the App, or clear its data/storage from Android Settings, this data is permanently deleted from your device.

Details of other people: if you record details of another person (for example, someone you lent money to), please make sure you are comfortable keeping those details. They stay only on your device, and we cannot see them.

PDF export: the App can create a PDF report of your entries or of your savings and loans. This only happens when you tap the export button. Android asks you where to save the file, and the App does not upload it anywhere. If you later share that file with someone, that is your own choice and outside the App.

4. PIN security

The 4-digit PIN you set is used only to lock access to the App's screens on your own device. The PIN is stored locally (as a one-way hash) and is never transmitted anywhere. The Savings & Loans section asks for the PIN again each time it is opened, and hides amounts until you choose to show them. The PIN restricts who can open the App on your phone; it does not encrypt the underlying data file. Please keep your device itself secured (for example, with a screen lock) for full protection.

5. Permissions

DailyHisaab does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, or storage beyond what Android automatically grants for the App's own private data folder. When you export a PDF, you pick the save location with Android's own file picker, which does not require any storage permission. If a future update requires a new permission, this policy will be updated to explain why.

6. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect or have access to any data in the first place. There are no third-party services integrated into this App that would receive your information.

7. Interest and loan calculations

The Savings & Loans section is a personal record-keeping tool. Interest, EMI, and balance figures are estimates calculated from what you enter. They are not financial, legal, or tax advice, and the App does not lend money, process payments, or contact anyone on your behalf. Loan and interest rules differ by country and state, so please follow the laws that apply to you.

8. Children's privacy

DailyHisaab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), because the App does not collect information from any user at all.

9. Your data, your control

Since all data stays on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • You can delete individual entries, savings records, loans, and payments at any time within the App.
  • You can clear all App data via Android Settings → Apps → DailyHisaab → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes in the future (for example, if a new feature adds cloud backup or ads), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
