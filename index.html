Privacy Policy

DailyHisaab — Daily Cash Book & Expense Tracker

Last updated: October 3, 2026

In short: DailyHisaab does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal or financial data to us or to any third party. Everything you enter — your income, expenses, savings, loans, bank account names, tour plans, PIN, and settings — stays only on your own device.

1. Who we are

DailyHisaab ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal daily income and expense tracking application for Android, developed by B Murali. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. DailyHisaab is designed to work completely offline. Specifically:

  • The App does not require an internet connection to function.
  • The App does not have any server, backend, or cloud database of its own.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, or have access to any data you enter in the App.
  • The App does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, or any third-party analytics or advertising SDK.
  • The App does not display advertisements.

3. Data you enter into the App

When you use DailyHisaab, you may enter the following information, which is stored only in local storage on your own device (using Android's local database, Room/SQLite):

  • Daily income and expense entries, amounts, categories, and remarks/notes you type
  • Savings records and loan records (money you lent or borrowed), including amounts, dates, interest rates, and payments
  • Optional details of the person on the other side of a loan, if you choose to type them: name, father's name, village, mandal, district, state, and mobile number. These are only the details you enter yourself; the App never reads your phone contacts
  • Names you give your own bank accounts (e.g. "SBI", "Union Bank") and, if you choose to enter it, that account's number — stored only so the App can show you each account's running balance
  • Tour Plan (optional feature): if you turn this on, you may enter group-trip details — the vehicle number and type, the driver's name and licence number, the trip treasurer's name, and for each traveller their name, village, and mobile number, along with the advance amount they gave and the trip's daily expenses. These are only the details you choose to type for your own record-keeping and settlement, and they stay fully separate from your daily cash book
  • A 4-digit PIN you set to lock the App
  • Your selected country, currency, and language preference
  • Your own optional profile (name, village, mobile number), shown only on PDF reports you generate yourself
  • Your choice to show or hide the Savings & Loans tab and the Tour Plan tab

This data never leaves your device on its own. It is not uploaded to any server, not shared with us, and not shared with any third party. If you uninstall the App, or clear its data/storage from Android Settings, this data is permanently deleted from your device.

Details of other people: if you record details of another person (someone you lent money to, a fellow traveller, or a driver), please make sure you are comfortable keeping those details. They stay only on your device, and we cannot see them.

PDF export: the App can create a PDF report of your cash book, your savings and loans, or a tour plan. This only happens when you tap the export button. Android asks you where to save the file, and the App does not upload it anywhere. If you later share that file with someone, that is your own choice and outside the App.

Manual backup & restore: Settings includes a "Backup now" option that packages all of the data listed above into a single file on your device, which you then choose where to send using Android's own share menu (for example, to your own email, to Google Drive, or over WhatsApp) — the App itself never sends or uploads this file anywhere. "Restore from backup" lets you pick such a file (for example on a new phone) to bring that data back into the App. Where that backup file is stored, and who can see it, is entirely up to the app or service you choose to save it to — please pick somewhere you trust, since the file contains the same personal and financial details described in this policy.

Your device's own backup: separately from the App, Android's built-in "Back up to Google Drive" device setting (if you have turned it on in your phone's own Settings) may back up this App's data to your personal Google account as part of backing up your whole phone. This is a feature of your Android device, not of the App, and is controlled entirely by your phone's Settings and your own Google account.

4. PIN security

The 4-digit PIN you set is used only to lock access to the App's screens on your own device. The PIN is stored locally (as a one-way hash) and is never transmitted anywhere. The Savings & Loans section asks for the PIN again each time it is opened, and hides amounts until you choose to show them. The PIN restricts who can open the App on your phone; it does not encrypt the underlying data file. Please keep your device itself secured (for example, with a screen lock) for full protection.

5. Permissions

DailyHisaab does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and does not ask for broad storage access beyond what Android automatically grants for the App's own private data folder. When you export a PDF or create a manual backup file, you pick the save location (or the app to send it through) using Android's own file picker and share menu, which do not require any special storage permission. If a future update requires a new permission, this policy will be updated to explain why.

6. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect or have access to any data in the first place. There are no third-party services integrated into this App that would receive your information. The only way data ever leaves your device is if you yourself choose to share a PDF report or a backup file through another app.

7. Interest, EMI and settlement calculations

The Savings & Loans section and the Tour Plan section are personal record-keeping tools. Interest, EMI, balance, and settlement figures are estimates calculated from what you enter. They are not financial, legal, or tax advice, and the App does not lend money, process payments, or contact anyone on your behalf. Loan and interest rules differ by country and state, so please follow the laws that apply to you.

8. Children's privacy

DailyHisaab does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), because the App does not collect information from any user at all.

9. Your data, your control

Since all data stays on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • You can delete individual entries, savings records, loans, payments, bank accounts, tours, travellers, and tour expenses at any time within the App.
  • You can clear all App data via Android Settings → Apps → DailyHisaab → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
  • You can make your own backup file at any time (Settings → Backup now) and store it wherever you choose.

10. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes in the future (for example, if a new feature adds cloud backup or ads), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
